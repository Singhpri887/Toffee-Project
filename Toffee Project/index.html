Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started, security, and migration.

How does Toffee integrate with existing AWS / GCP / Azure setups?

Toffee deploys as a lightweight sidecar or edge gateway via Terraform, Helm charts, or our 1-click CLI. It connects seamlessly to your existing VPC without requiring any breaking code changes or infrastructure rewrites.

Is Toffee fully compliant with SOC-2 Type II and HIPAA?

Yes. Toffee is SOC-2 Type II certified and provides end-to-end mTLS encryption, AES-256 data-at-rest encryption, and automated compliance logging suitable for healthcare (HIPAA) and fintech (PCI-DSS Level 1) applications.

What happens after my 14-day free trial ends?

During the 14-day trial, you get full unrestricted access to all Growth-tier features. No credit card is required to start. When the trial concludes, you can upgrade to a paid tier or transition to our forever-free sandbox tier.

Can we self-host Toffee on our own private Kubernetes cluster?

Yes, our Enterprise tier supports on-premise and air-gapped self-hosted Kubernetes deployments with dedicated engineering support and custom licensing models.
